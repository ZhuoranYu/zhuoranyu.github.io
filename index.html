Research Scientist · Netflix

Zhuoran Yu

I am a Research Scientist at Netflix, working on multimodal models and evaluation for video and visual content. My research interests include multimodal understanding and reasoning, data-centric learning, and benchmarks that reveal model capabilities and limitations.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UW–Madison, advised by Yong Jae Lee. Previously, I earned my master's in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, where I worked with Zsolt Kira.
